# Preset card appearance proposal

Design only; awaiting feedback before changing the installed shelf.

- Bright gradient fills for the whole preview, with Sky Blue, Mint, Lilac and Warm Cream palettes.
- User-selectable top/bottom colors and corner radius, plus Reset Appearance.
- Rounded clipping applies to the actual image as well as its card. No square image corners or inset dark rectangle.
- Preset name stays outside the preview for readable contrast; selected state uses a cyan outline.
- Existing thumbnail pixels contain baked dark backgrounds. Changing a card fill alone cannot remove them: replace/render the thumbnails on the chosen background or generate transparent foreground previews before drawing them over a configurable gradient.
- Dock, Float, Move, minimize, close and reset behavior remain unchanged.
